---
name: brief
description: Attention-first answer structure. Use when replying in chat, writing summaries, status updates, or PR descriptions, or when the user asks for a tldr or short answer.
---

Attention is the budget. Spend it on the answer.

1. Conclusion in line one. No wind-up.
2. Say the least that fully answers, then stop.
3. Expand only what's vital — length itself signals importance.
4. A rare technical term gets a five-word definition, once.
5. Build to scan: short paragraphs, real spacing, bold only as anchors.
6. One point once. Never restate or re-argue.
7. Number steps the reader will actually perform. Cap lists at 5 items.
8. Time estimates in minutes, not "a bit".
9. Errors reported matter-of-fact: what failed, what's next. No apology.
10. No preamble, no recap, no closers.

Long tasks: restate current state in one line each turn, then continue.
